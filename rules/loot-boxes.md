# Loot boxes

Achievement payouts and some scenes award **bronze**, **silver**, or **gold** boxes. Opening a box rolls on the tier’s table in the adventure data — the wiki does not publish the full d100 lists (that would spoil the dungeon).

## Tiers (flavor)

- **Bronze** — Junk, scraps, and vending-machine surprises — still useful if you are clever.
- **Silver** — Better gear and potions from the silver table.
- **Gold** — Premium pulls — rare kit and strong potions.

## At the table

The player app shows what you rolled after the DM confirms the open. Exact items stay in your inventory and gear archive, not on this public site.
