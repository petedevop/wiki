# Glossary

Short names for things you will see on the player app and in session.

## Crawler

Your PC on Floor Two — level, gear, deeds, and lore all hang off this identity.

## Deed

Something the table logged that counts toward achievements, sponsor taste, and evening scoring. You do not edit deeds on the wiki; they sync from play.

## Achievement wall

Trophy cards with hints. Progress shows on your phone first; the wiki exports **hints and tallies**, not the secret “for” text.

## Board bounty

A name from the **top ten** on the world leaderboard. Each session start the app rolls whether one of those names is “on the floor” tonight.

## Sponsor / booth

Evening brands that court crawlers based on deeds. Invites and contracts stay in the app until you earn them.

## Rival kitchen

An NPC your table turned into a Stadium opponent. Rivals reset when the evening resets.

## AI Favour

A once-per-roll token that counts as **advantage** on that check. You can hold at most one.

## Peek / live

**Peek** restores the world when the DM stops the app. **Live** is the real Tuesday table — do not expect the wiki snapshot to match mid-fight.
