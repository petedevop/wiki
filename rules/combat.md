# Combat on your phone

When a fight is active the player UI switches to combat mode in place — you do not navigate away from the table.

## On your turn

1. You may use **one optional skill** (open DC roll), then **attack**, **cast**, or **use an item**.
2. Attack, cast, and item use each **end your turn**.
3. **End turn** passes without acting.

## Anytime

- Off-turn trained skill rolls where the scene allows them.
- **My HP** drawer for self-care — does not spend your turn.

## What you do not see

- Exact foe HP (health bands only).
- DM-only tactics and hidden mechanics.

## Morale and retreat

When foes break, the DM runs morale in the app. **Retreat** uses the house table — your phone may prompt for a party vote on how you flee.

## Dying

At 0 HP you are **dying** until stabilized or healed. First aid is a trained check with a fixed DC in the app.
