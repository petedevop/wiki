# House rules

This table runs **Twilight Sword** on Radia, in **Aramilla Valley**. The published rulebook is not copied here — ask the DM or use the in-app **Library** PDFs on your phone.

Start with **[Champion rules (d12)](d12-champions)**. On each export the handbook also syncs numbers from the live app: **Champion rolls**, **Abilities**, **Ways & feats**, **Spells**, **Monster rotations**, **Cooking & brewing**, **Weapons & equipment**, **Travel & weather**, **Elements**, **Hope**, **Status effects**, **Phone combat**, **Rest & camp**, and more under Rules.

## How this table rolls

- Everything is **d12 roll-under** against your ability score — lower is better.
- Circumstance mods on one roll **cap at ±3**. Advantage and disadvantage are **2d12 keep lower / higher**; they cancel to a single tier.
- **Natural 1** = critical success; **natural 12** = critical failure.
- See **Champion rolls** and **Advantage & disadvantage** for the code-synced detail.

## The player app

- **Map** and **Archive** only show what the party has already discovered in play.
- **Combat** on the phone is turn-gated; the server enforces what you can tap — greyed buttons are not a suggestion.
- **Travel** is per champion. Stepping into a room that would **start combat** asks for confirmation first. **Party votes** need a majority of champions present.
- **Rest** is upstairs at the **Dancing Deer** (Room Key → guest room camp), or the short/long wilds rests the DM opens. Free **Cook & brew** buttons sit on the rest screen.
- Header shows **hearts** and **stamina**; combat may offer **dodge/parry** after foe damage; the **equipped pool** (three slots) is your combat-ready gear.
- House pages **Supplies & statuses** and **Phone combat** list ammo, debuff clocks, meat spoilage, corpse loot, and search-fail ambushes the server enforces.

## Aramilla Valley flavour

Champions hold the line against **the Scourge**. Green Zin is the coin of the valley. Treasure chests (bronze / silver / gold) open anywhere — see **Treasure chests**.
