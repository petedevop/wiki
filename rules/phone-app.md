# Using the player app

The **player app** is the site you open on your phone during session. Voice chat is for talking only — rolls, combat, inventory, and room text all happen here.

Layout: a **header** (portrait, vitals, statuses, stats, **quick slots** or **equipped pool**), the **room panel**, and bottom nav — **Home · Logs · Me · Notifications · Info · Settings**. During a cook-off you may also see **Kitchen Stadium** (🍳) until the show ends. With **[Champion rules (d12)](d12-champions)** on, the header shows **hearts** and **stamina** and three equipped slots instead of HP quick slots.

## Tap and hold

- **Tap** — do the thing (travel, cast, equip, open a sheet with actions).
- **Hold (~½ second)** — read the full text without acting: room **items** and people on the board, pack gear, **spells and class abilities** on Home, status chips (hold also opens the cause popover).

Status chips still **toggle** on tap; hold is extra detail, not a duplicate of tap.

## The header

- **Portrait** — green ring while you hold **AI Favour**; tap to arm it for your next roll.
- **HP chip** — tap to adjust HP (table rules permitting).
- **Status chips** — icons only; long-press for cause and time left. **Follower** is 👤, **mount** is 🏇. A **Following** chip appears while you trail another champion; tap it to stop.
- **Quick slots** — three tappable slots for potions, thrown weapons, and other combat-ready consumables. Tap to use; long-press to assign from the pack. The **trinket** neck slot is only for real trinkets and magic jewellery — not every odd consumable.
- **Ability strip** — each stat shows **score and modifier**, e.g. `14 (+2)`. Tap a stat for a freeform check_roll that lands in the activity log.
- **Room mates** — when other champions share your room, their faces appear across the header; tap one for a read-only peek at their sheet.

**Plot clocks** sit under the header band (not over the room picture).

## Home: the room panel

Between fights the room splits in two:

- **Left:** everyone and everything here — **people → obstacles → items** — with attitude, quest, and secret chips. **Tap** a row to act. **Hold** an item, obstacle, or NPC row to inspect lore and stats **without** acting; hold works the same on pack tiles. A **?** row is an unspotted secret; tap to search (failed party searches can eventually stir trouble — see **House rules → Supplies & statuses**). Someone with work for you lights in your colour with **QUEST** — tap for the offer; **Accept** or **Decline** (declined quests stay on that NPC with a **Read** option).
- **Right:** the **room picture** ( **Zone** / **Room** flips to the zone map), **Travel**, **Rest** / **Join combat** when they apply.

**Travel into a fight:** if the exit would start combat as soon as you step in, the app asks **Yes / No** on a bottom sheet before you move. **Go hostile** on someone in the room still starts a fight immediately.

## Moving, leading, and mounts

**Travel is per champion.** Pick an exit or tap a thumbnail on the zone map. **Gather** and **Follow** are in the travel sheet.

**Leading an NPC:** choose an exit that is actually open for you — sealed gates refuse with a clear message.

**Mount offers** do not spam after you refuse or already have a follower — new offers can include **swap** (ditch the current companion). A mount may carry a **bearing** slot for extra pack items. Riding a reluctant humanoid mount risks a bad mood once in a while when you change rooms.

## Solo rest

In a **safe room**, when you have enough food, a rest sheet can open (once per room visit on your phone). **Begin** spends **two** food units (rations, jerky, bread, or cooking ingredients — cheapest eaten first), runs a full personal recovery pass, then sleeps **about ten minutes** while travel and other actions are blocked. Combat in your room wakes you — healing already applied. Party **votes** for end-night, retreat, surrender, and scout are unchanged; the old party-vote “rest everyone” bar is gone from the phone.

## Logs and Notifications

- **Logs** — rolls and beats; filter **All** or **Me**.
- **Notifications** — deeds (gold lines now say **where** the gp came from), and **Quests** on one page.

## Info tab

Open **Info** for a **segment hub** (like Settings): tap a segment along the top — Archive, Map, Shop, Quest book, Projects, Party, **Library**, Rules, Hunters, and the rest. Your phone remembers which segment you had open.

| Segment | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| **Quest book** | Active quests and leads |
| **Archive** | Rooms, people, items, and obstacles **you** have discovered |
| **Map** | Zone map — pinch and drag |
| **Shop** | Sponsor booth when the table has one |
| **Projects** | Party jobs — stages and rewards |
| **Party** · **Crew** | Fellow champions and your followers — tap for sheets |
| **Library** | Haul credit and catalogue votes from Kitchen Stadium wins — tap entries to invoke when the table allows |

## Me tab

Your sheet: equipped weapon, **inventory** grid, spells, class abilities, and skills. **Hostage** (if you are dragging someone) has its own card: shield, trade back, execute, convert, or ally — see in-app labels.

**Followers** accept extra verbs: joust, feed, train, tricks, gear taps like your own pack, bond, turn hostile, and more — only what the companion supports.

## Inventory

Tap the pack line in the header: **Equipped** and **Pack**. Long-press gear for inspect-only. `↗` gives to another champion; `×` drops.

**During combat**, inventory stays open on your turn; the first change spends the turn (banner warns you).

## Kitchen Stadium

Tap the **Kitchen Stadium signpost** (or the DM starts the show) to enter the arena. While the cook-off runs, use the **🍳 tab** to leave and return without losing your place. Phases cover secret-ingredient fights, shared shelves, team checks, and Library credit scoring — score is haul credit, not rival drama.

Approach prose now calls out the **champion cooler**, service road, and loading docks so sneaking in is obvious in fiction.

## Combat

Fights replace the room panel: initiative strip, map, combat log, and your action menu. Use **quick slots** on your turn when you assigned them. See **Phone combat** in Rules for turn flow and **Solo flee** for leaving alone.

## Darkness and light

Some tunnels (e.g. **Bleacher Tunnel**) are **dark**. Without a held light source, searches and movement suffer; equip a torch or lantern in hand.

## Level-up

When you level, talent picks that add **+2 to one ability** apply to **that ability only** — not a second stat by mistake.

## Settings

Theme, text size, motion, contrast, alerts, pop-ups, **connection status**, and **Log out**.

## What the phone is not

- Not a character editor — the DM sheet is authoritative.
- Not a spoiler browser — archive and map only show what you have earned in play.
- Not this wiki. The wiki is a between-sessions snapshot; during the night, the app is live.

## Table closed (wiki fallback)

If the game Mac goes offline, you may land on the wiki with a short banner. It polls and sends you back — tap **Try now** if you do not want to wait.
