# Using the player app

The **player app** is the site you open on your phone during session. Voice chat is for talking only — rolls, combat, inventory, and room text all happen here.

Layout: a **header** (portrait, vitals, statuses, stats, **equipped pool**), the **room panel**, and bottom nav — **Home · Logs · Me · Notifications · Info · Settings**. Under **[Champion rules (d12)](d12-champions)** the header shows **hearts** and **stamina** and three equipped slots.

## Tap and hold

- **Tap** — do the thing (travel, cast, equip, open a sheet with actions).
- **Hold (~½ second)** — read the full text without acting: room **items** and people on the board, pack gear, **spells and class abilities** on Home, status chips (hold also opens the cause popover).

Status chips still **toggle** on tap; hold is extra detail, not a duplicate of tap.

## The header

- **Portrait** — your face on the strip.
- **Hearts / stamina** — vitals for the night.
- **Status chips** — icons only; long-press for cause and time left. **Follower** is 👤, **mount** is 🏇. A **Following** chip appears while you trail another champion; tap it to stop.
- **Equipped pool** — three combat-ready slots. Tap to use; long-press to assign from the pack. The **trinket** neck slot is only for real trinkets and magic jewellery.
- **Ability strip** — each of the eight stats shows its score. Tap a stat for a freeform d12 roll-under that lands in the activity log.
- **Room mates** — when other champions share your room, their faces appear across the header; tap one for a read-only peek at their sheet.

**Plot clocks** sit under the header band (not over the room picture).

## Home: the room panel

Between fights the room splits in two:

- **Left:** everyone and everything here — **people → obstacles → items** — with attitude and secret chips. **Tap** a row to act. **Hold** an item, obstacle, or NPC row to inspect lore and stats **without** acting; hold works the same on pack tiles. A **?** row is an unspotted secret; tap to search (failed party searches can eventually stir trouble — see **House rules → Supplies & statuses**).
- **Right:** the **room picture** ( **Zone** / **Room** flips to the zone map), **Travel**, **Rest** / **Join combat** when they apply.

**Travel into a fight:** if the exit would start combat as soon as you step in, the app asks **Yes / No** on a bottom sheet before you move. **Go hostile** on someone in the room still starts a fight immediately.

## Moving and mounts

**Travel is per champion.** Pick an exit or tap a thumbnail on the zone map. **Gather** and **Follow** are in the travel sheet when the table still offers them.

**Leading an NPC:** choose an exit that is actually open for you — sealed gates refuse with a clear message.

**Mount offers** do not spam after you refuse or already have a follower — new offers can include **swap** (ditch the current companion). A mount may carry a **bearing** slot for extra pack items.

## Rest

**Long rest** is upstairs at the **Dancing Deer**: buy a **Room Key** at the bar, use it in the guest room, take one camp action, then blow out the candles. **Cook & brew** are free buttons on the rest screen (not a camp action). Wilds short/long rests follow the same recovery pass when the DM opens them. See **Rest & camp** under Rules.

Party **votes** cover end-night, retreat, surrender, and scout.

## Logs and Notifications

- **Logs** — rolls and beats; filter **All** or **Me**.
- **Notifications** — deeds and table notices (gold lines name **where** the Green Zin came from).

## Info tab

Open **Info** for a **segment hub**: **Map · Activity · Archive · Party**. Your phone remembers which segment you had open.

| Segment | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| **Map** | Zone map — pinch and drag |
| **Activity** | Recent table beats |
| **Archive** | Rooms, people, items, and obstacles **you** have discovered |
| **Party** | Fellow champions — tap for sheets |

IMG|assets/guide/05-archive.png|Archive inside Info

## Me tab

Your sheet: equipped gear, **inventory** grid, spells, class abilities, and skills.

**Followers** accept extra verbs: feed, train, tricks, gear taps like your own pack, bond, turn hostile, and more — only what the companion supports.

IMG|assets/guide/07-me.png|Me — equipped weapon, inventory, spells and abilities

## Inventory

Tap the pack line in the header: **Equipped** and **Pack**. Long-press gear for inspect-only. `↗` gives to another champion; `×` drops.

**During combat**, inventory stays open on your turn; the first change spends the turn (banner warns you).

IMG|assets/guide/04-inventory.png|Inventory sheet

## Combat

Fights replace the room panel: initiative strip, map, combat log, and your action menu. Use the **equipped pool** on your turn. See **Phone combat** in Rules for turn flow and **Solo flee** for leaving alone.

## Darkness and light

Some rooms are **dark**. Without a held light source, searches and movement suffer; equip a torch or lantern in hand.

## Feats

Champions have **no character levels or XP**. Creation includes **one feat** from your Way. Each **4 party hope** (4, 8, 12, …) earns another class feat pick on the Me tab. **Second way** (once): take a second class and a feat from its list.

## Settings

Theme, text size, motion, contrast, alerts, pop-ups, **connection status**, and **Log out**.

## What the phone is not

- Not a character editor — the DM sheet is authoritative.
- Not a spoiler browser — archive and map only show what you have earned in play.
- Not this wiki. The wiki is a between-sessions snapshot; during the night, the app is live.

## Table closed (wiki fallback)

If the game Mac goes offline, you may land on the wiki with a short banner. It polls and sends you back — tap **Try now** if you do not want to wait.
