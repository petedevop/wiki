# Treasure chests

Foes and finds drop **Bronze**, **Silver**, or **Gold Treasure Chests**. Opening a chest rolls on that tier’s table in the adventure data — the wiki does not publish the full lists (that would spoil the finds). Chests open **anywhere**; you do not need a safe room.

## Tiers (flavor)

- **Bronze** — Everyday kit, potions, and clever junk.
- **Silver** — Better gear and stronger potions.
- **Gold** — Rare kit and premium pulls.

## At the table

The player app shows what you rolled after the open resolves. Exact items stay in your inventory and gear archive, not on this public site.

## Corpse search (monsters)

Searching a regular monster corpse (not a boss) rolls **50%** small pocket change **or** **50%** a **Bronze Treasure Chest** — never both. Bosses and special kills use the richer tier tables instead.
